# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This repository is a professional working environment. This policy sets the standard of
conduct expected of everyone who contributes to, reviews, or discusses the DevOS
documentation, whether they are an employee, contractor, or invited collaborator.

## Expected behaviour

- Engage with the substance of a contribution rather than the person who made it.
- Give specific, actionable review feedback, and state the reasoning behind it.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask before concluding that someone is wrong.
- Accept that documentation review is technical review. Being asked for evidence is normal.
- Respect decisions recorded through the RFC process, and use that process to revisit them
  rather than relitigating in unrelated threads.
- Be accurate. Do not overstate what a capability does, and do not let an inaccuracy stand
  because correcting it is awkward.
- Respect confidentiality. This repository contains internal specifications; do not share
  its contents outside the organisation without authorisation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind, including on the basis of race,
  ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual
  orientation, disability, age, or veteran status.
- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments about a contributor or their work.
- Sustained disruption of reviews or discussions, including repeatedly reopening settled
  decisions without new information.
- Publishing another person's private information without their consent.
- Sexualised language, imagery, or attention in any project space.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to all project spaces — pull requests, issues, commit messages,
reviews, RFC discussions, and any communication channel used to coordinate work on this
repository — and to conduct outside those spaces where it affects the working environment.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **conduct@macrotechtitan.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and
shared only with those who need to act on them.

If your concern involves the person who would normally receive it, contact any maintainer
of this repository directly, or use your usual internal escalation path.

Include what happened, when, where, and who was involved, along with any links or
screenshots. You do not need to be certain a policy was broken in order to report.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this policy and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that do not comply. Responses are proportionate and escalate with severity
and repetition:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining the problem and the expected change.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with defined consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary restriction** — time-limited removal of contribution or review access.
4. **Permanent removal** — removal of access to the repository and project spaces.

Serious violations, including harassment and retaliation, may result in permanent removal
on a first occurrence, and may be escalated through the appropriate internal channels.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this policy in good faith may face consequences
determined by the organisation's leadership.
