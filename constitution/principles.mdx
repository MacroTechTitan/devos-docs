## Purpose

These ten articles are binding on all DevOS product, engineering, and AI decisions. Each
states a requirement, the rationale, and how compliance is verified. Requirement keywords
follow RFC 2119 — see [Constitution: Introduction](/constitution/introduction).

The convictions behind these articles are discussed in
[Philosophy](/introduction/philosophy). This page is the enforceable form.

---

## Article 1 — Uncertainty is disclosed

Every recommendation, score, or generated artefact that rests on incomplete information
**MUST** disclose that incompleteness at the point of use.

DevOS **MUST NOT** present a value derived from a default, an inference, or a model guess as
though it were user-supplied fact. Where an input is unknown, the unknown **MUST** be
recorded as an unknown and propagated forward as a named evidence gap.

**Rationale.** A fluent answer that conceals its foundations is worse than a hedged one,
because it removes the reader's ability to judge it.

**Verification.** Every declared unknown in a product brief resolves to a named evidence gap
in the confidence review. A recommendation with no stated gaps must be demonstrably
gap-free, not merely silent.

---

## Article 2 — Decisions are immutable records

A decision, once accepted, **MUST NOT** be edited. A changed decision **MUST** be expressed
as a new record that supersedes the prior one, with the prior record retained and marked.

Every decision record **MUST** contain context, the decision, alternatives considered with
their rejection reasons, consequences, and the confidence at the time of acceptance.

**Rationale.** The history of reasoning is the asset. A mutable record is a setting, and a
project's architecture becomes archaeological rather than legible.

**Verification.** Supersession chains are complete and traversable. No storage operation
mutates an accepted record's substantive fields.

---

## Article 3 — Artefacts flow forward only

Each workflow stage **MUST** consume only the artefact immediately upstream, and **MUST NOT**
introduce a constraint absent from that input.

Where a downstream stage requires information its input lacks, the correct response is to
amend the upstream artefact through its own process. Patching the downstream artefact
directly **MUST NOT** be permitted.

**Rationale.** Sideways injection is how documented designs quietly stop describing the
system they claim to describe.

**Verification.** Every element of a downstream artefact resolves to a specific upstream
element. A blueprint element with no originating decision is a defect that blocks
compilation.

---

## Article 4 — Humans accept; systems recommend

Conversion of a recommendation into a decision **MUST** require an act by an identified
human, recorded with identity and timestamp.

No confidence score, threshold, or configuration **MAY** authorise automatic acceptance.

**Rationale.** Accountability is what makes the ledger worth consulting. A ledger of machine
output records what a system did, not what anyone committed to.

**Verification.** Every decision record has exactly one accepting human identity. No code
path creates an accepted record without one.

---

## Article 5 — Status is declared honestly

Every capability **MUST** declare exactly one status: Concept, Planned, Prototype, Private
beta, Beta, or Production.

Unbuilt behaviour **MUST NOT** be described in the present tense, in documentation, in the
product interface, or in any generated artefact. Status promotion **MUST** be supported by
linked evidence.

**Rationale.** Status honesty is what allows a reader to trust anything else on the page.

**Verification.** Page status matches the authoritative capability table. Promotions link to
a demo, test run, or release note.

---

## Article 6 — Frameworks name their failure modes

Every framework in the library **MUST** state when it is the wrong choice, what breaks first
under stress, and the conditions under which it should be abandoned.

A framework that documents only its strengths **MUST NOT** be published.

**Rationale.** A reference architecture exists to support a choice. Strengths alone cannot
discriminate between candidates.

**Verification.** Each framework page contains a populated "When not to use this" section
and named failure modes. See [Framework standard](/frameworks/framework-standard).

---

## Article 7 — Tenant isolation is absolute

No operation **MAY** read, write, export, or retrieve data across an organisation boundary.
This applies equally to user-initiated queries, background jobs, analytics, support access,
and AI agent retrieval.

Cross-organisation access **MUST NOT** be implemented as a permission that can be granted.

**Rationale.** Isolation that is configurable is isolation that will eventually be
misconfigured.

**Verification.** Every data access path is scoped at the organisation boundary by
construction. Agent retrieval contexts are organisation-scoped before retrieval, not
filtered after. See [Multi-tenancy](/architecture/multi-tenancy).

---

## Article 8 — Agents operate under explicit contracts

Every AI agent **MUST** have a documented contract stating its permitted inputs, its output
schema, its authority limits, and its escalation path.

An agent **MUST NOT** accept a decision, alter a ledger record, widen its own scope, or act
outside its declared authority. Agent output **MUST** be attributable to the agent, the
model, and the invoking context.

**Rationale.** Agents are useful in proportion to how bounded they are.

**Verification.** Each agent has a published contract conforming to
[Agent standard](/agents/agent-standard). Outputs carry attribution metadata.

---

## Article 9 — Prefer inspectable mechanisms

Where two mechanisms satisfy a requirement, DevOS **SHOULD** choose the one a reviewer can
verify by reading it.

Confidence scoring, ledger state transitions, and artefact compilation **MUST** be
deterministic and auditable given the same inputs. Emergent or opaque behaviour **MUST NOT**
be used for these three.

**Rationale.** DevOS asks users to trust its reasoning. Reasoning that cannot be inspected
cannot be trusted, and cannot be debugged when it is wrong.

**Verification.** Compilation is reproducible. Ledger transitions form a small enumerated
state machine. Scoring inputs and weights are recorded with each score.

---

## Article 10 — Documentation is part of the system

This documentation set **MUST** be maintained as a functional component, not as
commentary.

Headings are part of the retrieval contract for AI agents; renaming one **MUST** be treated
as a breaking change requiring a redirect or a version history note. Normative pages
**MUST** carry a version history. Documentation that contradicts implemented behaviour
**MUST** be corrected with the same urgency as a product defect.

**Rationale.** DevOS agents retrieve from this repository. Inaccurate documentation is a
runtime fault, not an editorial lapse.

**Verification.** Normative pages carry version histories. Heading changes appear in version
history. No page describes behaviour contradicting the authoritative status table.

---

## Applying the articles

Most review disputes reduce to four questions:

| Question | Article |
| --- | --- |
| Is unbuilt behaviour described in the present tense? | 5 |
| Can a downstream artefact contain a choice no human accepted? | 3, 4 |
| Is an unknown being silently defaulted? | 1 |
| Can any path cross an organisation boundary? | 7 |

## Deviations

Articles stated with **MUST** admit no deviation. Articles stated with **SHOULD** permit a
deviation recorded in the relevant decision record, naming the article, the reason, and the
accepted consequence. An undocumented deviation is a defect regardless of its merit.
